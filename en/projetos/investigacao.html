Information Retrieval and Mining for Investigation Domains


This project began in March 2006, partially funded by CNPq through two RHAE grants (process no. 520092/06-6) and carried out by the Critical Technology Applications Foundation — Atech. It was fully maintained and carried out by Atech until October 2009.

Objective

The project's objective was to develop an information-retrieval and mining system to support investigative work. The system processes diverse information sources and identifies relationships among retrieved items, such as documents and database records. Evidence of these relationships is presented through several means, including graphical representations that allow investigators to build visual overviews of ongoing investigations, explore data interactively, and monitor changes in investigative scenarios.

The keywords for this work are:

  1. Information retrieval and mining;
  2. Ontology-based query expansion;
  3. Named-entity recognition;
  4. Clustering algorithms; and
  5. Techniques for visualizing patterns in social networks.


Outputs

The outputs developed in this project were:

  1. Ariadne System: a system owned and marketed by the Critical Technology Applications Foundation — Atech. For more information, please send me an email or contact Atech.

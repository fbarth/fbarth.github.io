Socioeconomic Impacts of Artificial Intelligence


This research area investigates the social and economic impacts of developing and adopting Artificial Intelligence systems. The work discusses how these technologies transform work, social relationships, and well-being, considering both their benefits and their risks and challenges.

Materials:

  1. Agents and the future of data science: from analysis to orchestration, the data scientist's new role Introductory material for the panel “Intelligent agents: strategy and results,” organized by Insper and TCS.

  2. MOREIRA, F. G.; BARTH, FABRÍCIO. O Impacto da Era Digital na Saúde Mental In: Saúde Mental, ed.1. São Paulo: Sanar, 2025, p. 1 - 664.

  3. Impacts of Machine Learning on society. Talk given at several events throughout 2019.
